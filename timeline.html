Case Timeline

A selected chronology of established dates that may help readers place testimony, records, and investigative steps in context. This timeline is intentionally not exhaustive.

DateEventSource
December 28, 2020 Birchmore reported learning that she was pregnant. October 19 — Special Agent Heidi Kelsch testimony
December 29, 2020 Birchmore and Farwell met. Messages introduced at trial later that day described Birchmore as shaking and scared after the encounter. October 19 — Special Agent Heidi Kelsch testimony
January 20, 2021 A friend of Birchmore called the Stoughton Police Department about Birchmore and Matthew Farwell. Dispatcher James Fitton later testified that he relayed the call to Farwell. October 20 — James Fitton testimony
February 1, 2021 · 9:14 PM Apartment surveillance showed Farwell entering Birchmore’s apartment building. October 19 — Special Agent Heidi Kelsch testimony
February 1, 2021 · 9:40 PM Birchmore’s iPhone activity sensor recorded eight steps, identified in testimony as the phone’s final recorded movement. October 19 — Special Agent Heidi Kelsch testimony
February 4, 2021 Canton Police conducted a welfare check at Birchmore’s apartment and found her deceased. October 13 — Officer Ensley Cotard testimony
February 5, 2021 Dr. María del Mar Capó Martínez performed Birchmore’s autopsy. October 26 — Dr. Capó Martínez testimony
February 6, 2021 Massachusetts State Police interviewed Matthew Farwell as part of the investigation into Birchmore’s death. October 14 — Trooper Matthew Dunne testimony
February 9, 2021 Farwell consented to a data extraction of his personal iPhone. October 15 — Nicholas Guarino testimony
February 9, 2021 Investigators retrieved Birchmore’s Apple laptop for data collection and preservation. October 15 — Nicholas Guarino testimony
February 26, 2021 At police request, Dr. Capó Martínez performed a sexual-assault evidence collection kit. October 26 — Dr. Capó Martínez testimony
April 26, 2021 Stoughton Police delivered Farwell’s department-issued iPhone to Norfolk County investigators, where it was logged as evidence. October 16 — Brian Holmes testimony
May 6, 2021 Dr. Capó Martínez signed the final OCME report listing the cause of death as asphyxia by hanging and the manner of death as suicide. October 26 — Dr. Capó Martínez testimony
May 21, 2021 Birchmore’s death certificate formally recorded the cause of death as asphyxia by hanging and the manner of death as suicide. Death certificate / Norfolk DA records
August 29, 2022 Stoughton Police concluded its internal-affairs investigation and released a redacted report concerning Matthew Farwell, William Farwell, and Robert Devine. October 16 — Brian Holmes testimony / Stoughton IA report
August 28, 2024 A federal grand jury indicted Matthew Farwell on one count of killing a witness or victim. Farwell was arrested the same day and made his initial federal court appearance. U.S. Attorney’s Office, District of Massachusetts
April 30, 2026 The OCME amended Birchmore’s death certificate. The cause of death was changed to asphyxia and the manner of death to undetermined. October 26 — Dr. Capó Martínez testimony

Source note: Entries are drawn from testimony and records reflected in the Grant Smith Ellis court-reporting archive, with an official federal source used for the August 28, 2024 charging event. Inclusion is for chronological orientation only and does not characterize the importance, credibility, or weight of an event or item of evidence. Events sharing a date are listed separately; proximity in the table should not be read as establishing a connection or causal relationship. Times are included only where testimony placed multiple events within a narrow time window and the time materially aids orientation.
