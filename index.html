About this archive

Method, corrections & attribution

Method. Each court-day document is treated as a self-contained publication unit. Grant Smith Ellis’s chronological reporting forms the primary record presented there, with links or identifiers for the original reporting retained wherever practical.

Corrections. If you spot a factual or source-link problem, identify the court day and, where possible, the specific entry involved. Corrections can be submitted through the repository’s Issues page.

Attribution. Courtroom reporting attributed to Grant Smith Ellis remains his work. Editorial organization, navigation, source annotation, and archive structure are separate from the underlying reporting.
