# Sandra Birchmore Trial Files

## GSE Court Reporting Archive

A public reading archive for chronological courtroom reporting by **Grant Smith Ellis (GSE)** from *United States v. Matthew Farwell*, the federal prosecution arising from the death of Sandra Birchmore.

> **Important:** These pages are **not official court transcripts**. They are a reading-oriented reconstruction of contemporaneous courtroom reporting. Original reporting remains attributable to Grant Smith Ellis; this archive is intended to make that reporting easier to navigate chronologically and preserve source provenance.

## Method

The initial archive is deliberately simple. GSE's chronological reporting forms the primary record presented here. Entries should retain links or identifiers for the original reporting wherever practical.

The archive may later expand to include publicly released trial files, exhibits, official transcripts, or clearly separated supplemental reporting if those materials become useful. Their absence here should not be interpreted as absence from the court record.

## Corrections

Factual corrections to the archive can be submitted through this repository's Issues page. Corrections should identify the trial day and, where possible, the specific entry or source involved.

## Attribution

Courtroom reporting attributed to Grant Smith Ellis remains his work. Editorial organization, navigation, source annotation, and archive structure are separate from the underlying reporting.
